The state, nation by nation

Every open public body associated with each nation, split into the three branches of the state. A nation is shown only where a register states it, or where the body answers to that nation's legislature.

Bodies answering to the UK Parliament that state no territorial extent are shown under “UK Government, extent not stated”, rather than guessed as England-only. The top of each branch is stated from its founding legislation, linked; the legislatures themselves are not yet register records. Court administration and judicial appointments bodies are counted with the judiciary, in their own tier, although they do not judge cases. 674 open bodies have no nation in any source and are not shown.
