      Org charts of the UK state

      Every public body in its context, four ways round. Select a box to open or close it (charts run top to bottom; switch to left to right for wide trees); drag to move around; use the buttons or your mouse wheel to zoom. Each body links to its full profile, with its sources, rules, website and relationships. Same-as duplicates are shown once. Where a body works across more than one nation, the nation view shows it in each.
