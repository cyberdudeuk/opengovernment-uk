The state, nation by nation

Every open public body associated with each nation, in the three branches of the state, as a list by tier or as a parent-and-child hierarchy. Each placement shows its evidence: who sponsors the body, what it says about itself, its founding Act, or the register that states it.

How a nation is decided

Evidence is ranked, and a weaker source never overrides a stronger one; disagreements are listed below, not hidden. In order: the legislature the body answers to; its sponsor as ONS records it; what the body's own GOV.UK page says; the nation its register states; ONS local authority codes; the extent of its founding Act; Wikidata; a 2020 compilation of public bodies; where it is registered as a company. Nothing is guessed from a name. Bodies answering to the UK Parliament with no evidence of extent are shown as “UK Government, extent not stated”. Court administration and judicial appointments bodies are counted with the judiciary, in their own tier. The top of each branch is stated from its founding legislation, linked.
