# Glossary and thesaurus

| Term | Meaning | Also called (thesaurus) |
|---|---|---|
| Accountability chain | The steps from a body to the elected body it answers to, each with its source | chain, line of accountability |
| Accountable owner | The body at the top of the chain, below the elected body | top body, root body |
| Arm's-length body (ALB) | A body that delivers a public function at a distance from ministers | ALB, public body |
| Back-office body (class A) | A body that exists to run a function for government itself | internal body, corporate function body |
| Back-office line of business (class B) | A public-facing body that also runs a cross-government back-office service | shared service line |
| BODS | Beneficial Ownership Data Standard, used here at version 0.4 | beneficial ownership standard |
| COFOG | UN Classification of the Functions of Government | function classification |
| Concordance | One row per body with every identifier it has in every register | crosswalk, identifier map |
| Estimates and Accounts Order (EAO) | The order that designates which bodies a department's Estimate and accounts include | designation order |
| Executive agency | A part of a department with its own management, reporting to ministers | agency |
| Fingerprint | The SHA-256 of a source file, recorded when fetched | hash, checksum |
| Interest (Canadian sense) | A holding or appointment right short of control | minority interest |
| Non-departmental public body (NDPB) | A body with a role in government but not part of a department | quango |
| Non-ministerial department | A department headed by officials, not ministers; terminal in chains | NMD |
| PFI / PPP | Private finance initiative; public-private partnership | private finance |
| Public corporation | A body controlled by government that sells goods or services at market prices | state-owned enterprise |
| Safe-fail matching | Joining names only on exact evidence, leaving gaps rather than guessing | conservative matching |
| Trading fund | A body allowed to fund its operations from its own revenue under the Government Trading Funds Act 1973 | — |
| Crown Dependency | The Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey (with Alderney and Sark). They are not part of the UK and make their own laws; the UK is responsible for their defence and international relations, and the Ministry of Justice manages the relationship. | Crown dependency; Isle of Man, Jersey, Guernsey |
| Overseas Territory | One of the 14 territories under UK sovereignty but outside the UK, such as Bermuda or the Falkland Islands. Each has its own constitution, usually set out in an Order in Council. | British Overseas Territory; BOT |
| Founding order | The Order in Council or Act that sets up a territory's constitution and its law-makers, government and courts. We read it on legislation.gov.uk and quote the clause that creates each body. | constitution order; Order in Council |
| Combined record | One body that different sources record under different names or keys. We show it once, with every source's identifiers together, and say which rule joined them. | same-as; duplicate record |
| Decided from evidence | A disagreement between sources that the automatic rules could not settle, decided by a person from the body's own page or the law that set it up. The evidence is kept with the decision. | manual reconciliation; human decision |
| Single point of contact | The one UK body that another country or an international organisation deals with on a subject | SPOC; national focal point; National Central Bureau |
| UK mission or delegation | A team of UK officials posted to an international organisation | permanent representation; UKMis; UKDel |
