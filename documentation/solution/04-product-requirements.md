# Product requirements document

**Vision.** An open, evidenced register of the UK state that anyone can use and improve: what exists, who answers for it, who pays and who owns.

**Goals for 0.4**
1. People get from a name to an evidenced answer in under a minute, on any device (OG-R35).
2. Comparator best practice adopted: founding legislation, COFOG function, movements, interests, parent history (OG-R37).
3. Contributions reviewed in public within a week (OG-R33).

**Non-goals.** Named people; opinions on whether a body should exist; real-time data; anything needing a server, sign-in or paid service.

**Success measures** (measured without tracking users)

| Measure | How | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Chains established | Share of open bodies with an evidenced chain | ≥ 91% (now), rising |
| Conflicts and gaps visible | Every conflict and unresolved name listed with a reason | 100% |
| Correction turnaround | Days from issue to merge, from GitHub | ≤ 7 days |
| Standards validity | BODS schema errors | 0 |

**Requirements.** The full list, with priorities, status and traceability, is generated in 05-requirements-specification.md and browsable in the navigator.
