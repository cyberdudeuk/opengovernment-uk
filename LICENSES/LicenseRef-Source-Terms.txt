Source terms

Each file under sources/evidence/ holds facts read from a named source on a stated date: its URL, the date it was
read and, where the source states one, its licence, are recorded in the file itself. Those facts remain under the
source's own terms. Examples: Wikidata (CC0 1.0), GLEIF (CC0 1.0), GOV.UK, legislation.gov.uk and other UK public
sector sources (Open Government Licence v3.0), and World Bank Group Finances One datasets (CC BY 4.0). Where a source
states no licence (for example a member list on an international organisation's website), only short factual items
needed to cite and check the portal's data are kept, with a link back to the source.

The arrangement of these files, and any notes added to them, are by OpenGovernment UK contributors under the Open
Government Licence v3.0.
