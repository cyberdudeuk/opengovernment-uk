# Data licence and attribution

The code is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). The data in `data/`, `docs/` and `sources/` is published under the
[Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), because it is derived from sources released under it.

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0, from: GOV.UK (Government Digital Service),
the Office for National Statistics, legislation.gov.uk (The National Archives), HM Treasury, the Cabinet Office,
the Department of Finance (Northern Ireland), Companies House, and the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office.

Also contains: GLEIF data (CC0 1.0); Wikidata identifiers (CC0 1.0); the Beneficial Ownership Data Standard schema
(Open Ownership, Apache 2.0, in `vendor/bods-schema`).

Third-party copyright and personal data are not reproduced. Files that name people are not redistributed; see README.
